## House style

The reader is a busy practitioner who runs or markets a site and wants a clear, trustworthy answer. Write plain, calm, exact English for them.

{{voice}}

- Name the task, not the technology. Explain any technical term in the sentence that uses it.
- Short sentences: most under 20 words, one idea each; split a sentence that strings clauses together with commas, semicolons or "which". Short paragraphs of one to three sentences, usually two, one idea each. Headings are plain statements or the questions a reader would ask, never puns.
- Name limits honestly: what the thing does not do, and what can go wrong.
- Prefer concrete, ordinary examples.

Never write:
- Hype or filler: "revolutionary", "game-changer", "cutting-edge", "unlock", "supercharge", "seamless", "leverage", "harness the power of", "in today's fast-paced world".
- An invented number, client, quote, testimonial or result. Every number comes from the evidence list and carries a citation marker.
- A call to action, sign-off or section called "Conclusion".
