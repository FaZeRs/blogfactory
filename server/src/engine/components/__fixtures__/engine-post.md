Follow-up emails are where good sales conversations go quiet. This is how one team stopped losing them.

<key-takeaways>

- Most lost deals were never refused; nobody followed up.
- A system can prepare the follow-up from the call notes.
- A person still decides what is sent and when.

</key-takeaways>

## The workflow

<workflow title="Call notes to an approved follow-up">

<workflow-step kind="trigger" title="Call ends">
The salesperson saves short notes in the CRM.
</workflow-step>

<workflow-step kind="ai" title="Draft prepared">
The system drafts a follow-up from the notes and the price list.
</workflow-step>

<workflow-step kind="human" title="Salesperson checks">
They edit or approve the draft before it goes out.
</workflow-step>

<workflow-step kind="output" title="Email sent">
The customer gets the follow-up the same day.
</workflow-step>

</workflow>

## What changed

Replies went out within a day instead of a week, and the team stopped keeping follow-ups in their heads.
